Custom animation · GLSL, all screens

Define fhWarp (where to sample the image) and/or fhColor (final color). uv is 0..1 across the design, t is seconds, m is the cursor in uv, h is 1 while hovering. imageAt(uv) reads your image. Code that fails to compile is ignored; the browser console says why.

Add it to your site

One dependency-free script, 29 KB minified, about 12 KB over the wire. It draws into a canvas behind your hero content and reacts to the cursor anywhere over the section.

If your hero already has a background video, keep it inside the section with data-fluted-fallback. The script hides it once WebGL starts, so devices without WebGL still see the video.

  • Tablet and mobile settings switch on by the hero's width, like CSS max-width media queries; Mobile builds on Tablet
  • Pauses when the hero scrolls out of view or the tab is hidden
  • Shows a still frame for visitors who prefer reduced motion
  • Lowers its own resolution on slow graphics cards
